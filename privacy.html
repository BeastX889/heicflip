Heicflip Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026 · LumioLab

Short version: your photos never leave your device. Heicflip has no accounts and no servers that see your photos.

Your photos

Every photo you open in Heicflip is converted entirely on your own computer. Photos are not uploaded anywhere, and LumioLab never sees them. When you choose to keep photo details, the date, camera and lens information is copied from each original into its converted copy on your device. Location is removed from the copies unless you turn that option off.

What is stored on your device

  • Your last-used settings (format, quality, size, theme), kept in the app’s local storage on this device.
  • The app’s own files, cached so it works offline.

Optional tips

Heicflip has no ads and no tracking. In the Microsoft Store version you can leave an optional tip. Tips are paid through the Microsoft Store: Microsoft handles the payment, and Heicflip never sees your payment details. The app only learns that a tip went through, so it can say thank you.

Information LumioLab collects

None. LumioLab does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data or analytics. Heicflip converts photos with no internet connection at all.

Children

Heicflip is not directed at children under 13 and LumioLab does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
